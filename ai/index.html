Agentic Bug Management

Claude agents scan a dozen-plus repositories, from backend services to front-end clients, log what they find as ranked Jira tickets, and open a pull request with a proposed fix for each, giving a team one prioritized list to review and burn down each quarter. The first pass logged more than 150 bugs. A live demo app replays the workflow on simulated findings.

Homepage Dashboard

A private dashboard for my homelab, configured heavily with AI. Rich cards show live numbers from every service, bookmarks managed in Karakeep are synced to the bottom of the page, and a cron job keeps it current every 15 minutes. It sits behind a VPN, so the write-up uses edited screenshots.
